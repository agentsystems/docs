# Welcome to AgentSystems

AgentSystems is a platform for running AI agents on your infrastructure.

## What is AgentSystems?

AgentSystems allows you to:
- Deploy and run AI agents locally or on your servers
- Connect to various AI model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama)
- Maintain control over your data and credentials
- Build and share custom agents

## Core Principles

- **Data Sovereignty**: Your data stays on your infrastructure
- **Model Flexibility**: Use any supported AI provider
- **Container Isolation**: Agents run in isolated Docker containers
- **Open Source**: Transparent, auditable codebase
