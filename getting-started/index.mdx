## The Open Platform for AI Agents

AgentSystems lets you run community and commercial AI agents in any cloud or on your own hardware. Container isolation and egress controls constrain what agents can do.

<Info>
**What is AgentSystems?** An open platform that runs AI agents in Docker containers, supports multiple AI providers, and includes audit logging capabilities. Deploy on your infrastructure or in your cloud.
</Info>

## Why Use an Open Platform for AI Agents?

Stop sending your data to AI services. Run agents on your own machines or in your own cloud:
- **Data Sovereignty**: Process data on your infrastructure
- **Provider Flexibility**: OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock, or local models via Ollama
- **Cost Control**: Use your hardware and API keys directly
- **Audit Trail**: Hash-chained logging for operation tracking

## Use Cases

The platform targets scenarios requiring local AI execution:

- Processing sensitive documents
- Running AI workloads on-premise
- Keeping data within specific infrastructure
- Testing multiple AI providers
- Building containerized AI agents
