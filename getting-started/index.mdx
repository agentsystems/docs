## A Step Towards AI Sovereignty

AgentSystems is an open-source platform for running containerized AI agents on your infrastructure with control over your data and AI provider choices.

<Info>
**What is AgentSystems?** A self-hosted platform that runs AI agents in Docker containers, supports multiple AI providers, and includes audit logging capabilities.
</Info>

## The Platform Approach

AgentSystems addresses common AI deployment challenges:
- **Data Location**: Process data on your own infrastructure
- **Provider Flexibility**: Support for OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock, and Ollama
- **Cost Visibility**: Run agents on your hardware with your API keys
- **Audit Requirements**: Hash-chained logging for tracking operations

## Use Cases

The platform targets scenarios requiring local AI execution:

- Processing sensitive documents
- Running AI workloads on-premise
- Keeping data within specific infrastructure
- Testing multiple AI providers
- Building containerized AI agents
